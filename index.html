Calculation Logic

  • If two consecutive interactions are 10 minutes or less apart, they count as the same session; actual elapsed time is summed.
  • If the gap is more than 10 minutes, the session ends; the gap is not counted (as if you logged out and back in).
  • All sources (login, likes, comments, messages, saved, searches...) are connected as checkpoints.
If two interactions are within 10 minutes, they count as one session; otherwise the gap is excluded.

Step-by-Step Instagram Data Download Guide

1

Go to Accounts Center

Open the Instagram app, go to your profile, tap the three lines (menu icon) in the top right, then select Settings and privacy → Accounts Center.

2

Download Your Information

On the Your information and permissions page, follow the Download your information path. Select your account and click on Access your information. All data should be selected in the dropdown menu.

3

Export Type: All Info (recommended)

On the "Download or transfer info" screen, select "Some of your information" and tap the "Select all" button. Or check all of the following with "Choose specific info":

Comments
Messages
Likes
Saved
Stories
Searches
Security and login information
↳ Technical information and login activity
↳ Login and profile creation
Information about you
↳ Profile information

All of them are required! The app works best with login_activity.json plus comments, messages, likes, stories, and searches; skipping a source means the duration estimate will be incomplete.

4

Request Format: All time (recommended)

On the Date range screen, select All time. This ensures your entire history from account creation is analyzed. The note "may take longer to export" is fine, it's worth the wait.

5

Format: JSON

Make sure the export format is JSON (not HTML, the app does not read that). If you see multiple JSON files, pick them all.

6

Submit Your Request

Click the Submit request button. Instagram will prepare your data and send a download link to your email. Preparation time can vary from a few minutes to a couple of hours.

7

Download the ZIP and Import It

Download the ZIP archive from the email to your computer. No extraction needed, just drag and drop the entire ZIP here. The app finds login_activity.json in the archive and processes it automatically.

Tip: Do not close the app while waiting. Get the link, download the ZIP, and drop it directly here. All analysis is done 100% locally in your browser, no server involved.
